Our Story

Who We Are

We are 8 passionate young people from Alangulam, Tenkasi District — united by one belief: no student should lose their future simply because no one showed them the way.

ENRISERS was born from a simple question: "What if someone had guided us earlier?" We visit schools, spend one hour with students, and open their eyes to the possibilities ahead — completely free of charge.

We don't just talk. We listen, discover strengths, and walk alongside every student who needs long-term mentorship — in Engineering, Medicine, Arts, Fine Arts, and every field in between.

Inspired by the life and vision of Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam, we believe that the fire of a guided dream can light up not just one life, but an entire community.

🌱

When one youth rises → the family rises → the community prospers → society transforms.

Our Pillars

What We Do

Five pillars that hold up every student's future.

🎯

Career Guidance

We visit schools and conduct free 1-hour career awareness sessions, introducing students to pathways they never knew existed.

🤝

Mentorship

We identify students who need deeper support and provide long-term mentorship across Engineering, Medicine, Arts, and all career fields.

💡

Skill Development

Workshops focused on communication, AI literacy, and personal development — skills that schools rarely teach but life always demands.

🌐

Opportunity Connect

We bridge students with scholarships, programs, competitions, and real-world opportunities that match their strengths and aspirations.

🏘️

Community Building

We build a network of rising youth who support, inspire, and lift each other — because no one should climb alone.
